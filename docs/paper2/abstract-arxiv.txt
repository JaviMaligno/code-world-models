In the Code World Model paradigm an LLM synthesizes an executable world model that a classical planner searches, and the model is accepted when it reproduces sampled transitions. We ask what that acceptance certifies in continuous control. We define the pipeline's danger as an expected risk and isolate its exact factor: the probability that N i.i.d. gate rollouts all miss a critical event of probability r is exactly (1-r)^N; with an acceptance sample independent of training, the miss exponent of the central event depends on the two budgets only through their sum. On three hybrid instruments (cart with wall, pendulum with stop, 4D bi-modal patch field) the accepted mode-blind model is not merely wrong but exploited: the planner is pinned at the mode boundary at a regret of essentially the whole attainable return. We prove a localization budget: models with Lipschitz constant at most L differing by eta at a point must disagree above tolerance eps on volume at least ((eta-eps)/L)^(d+m); hybrid discontinuities pay no such budget. With real LLM synthesis, GPT-5.x repairs an omitted 1D clamp exactly in 105 of 111 mode-containing draws; on 2D regions no artifact recovers the rule (0/156; two small cross-family spot-checks fail alike), eight targeted interventions leave the failure in place -- one raising the evidence's coverage until a three-line fit recovers the region on every sample -- and positive controls locate it: a located rule is not induced, while given form and location the constants follow exactly. We prove a class of entry rules exactly consistent with every sample yet harmless at play, making identifiability a measurable property of the instrument. Re-scoring all 1034 artifacts on independent samples confirms acceptance certifies sample consistency and no more: where the gate is provably informative it covers about two percent of the exploited planner's queries.
